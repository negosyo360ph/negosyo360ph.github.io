Everything a small business runs on

For sari-sari stores, retail shops, food businesses, online sellers, resellers and freelancers.

Sales & POS

Tap or scan items. Cash with change, GCash, Maya, bank or utang. Discounts, receipts and refunds.

Inventory

Stock in with suggested quantities, barcode scanning with your phone camera and low-stock alerts.

Expenses & profit

Record bills and supplies. See sales, cost of goods and estimated profit for today, this week or this month.

Cash drawer

Know how much cash should be in your drawer. Count it and see right away if you are short or over.

Utang list

Who owes you, how much, and when it's due. Record partial payments and send reminders by Messenger or SMS.

Ask Negosyo

Ask "What should I reorder?" or "Why is my profit lower?" and get answers from your own numbers.

How to install on Android

Download the APK

Tap "Download for Android" on your phone.

Allow the install

Open the file. If asked, allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser.

Set up your store

Enter your business name, add products and record your first sale.

Using an iPhone? Open the web app in Safari and tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
